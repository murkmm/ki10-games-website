# Ki10 Games Ltd — QA Service Terms (DRAFT — not published or in force)

**Important:** Working draft for review before it is supplied to clients or linked from the site. These terms need checking against the company's registered details, VAT status, insurance/contracting position, and whether a customer is buying as a business or consumer. Do not assume these terms waive statutory consumer rights.

## 1. Parties and engagement
Ki10 Games Ltd ("Ki10", "we", "us") supplies manual game quality-assurance testing to the client identified in the written quotation ("client"). These terms only apply if the client accepts the quotation and these terms in writing. The quotation takes priority in case of inconsistency.

## 2. The agreed work
The quotation identifies the game, build/version, platform(s), test focus, total hours **including reporting**, deliverables, fee, payment terms and target delivery date. Work outside the quoted scope (new builds, retesting, certification, automated testing, fixes and ongoing support) is not included unless specifically agreed in writing. If the supplied build cannot run on agreed hardware, we will discuss alternative work or rescheduling before using booked testing time.

## 3. Testing limitations
Ki10 will perform the agreed services with reasonable care and skill. Testing is time-limited and risk-based. We do not promise to identify every defect, guarantee a bug-free game, or guarantee platform/store approval. We will not invent defects or promise a minimum finding count. Reports distinguish reproducible defects from design or usability observations.

## 4. Client responsibilities
The client must have permission to share the build and the materials needed for testing, including any necessary test accounts. The client will provide access and requirements in a reasonable time, and flag any special safety or confidentiality restrictions. The client is responsible for deciding which findings to fix and whether/when to release.

## 5. Quotes and payment
Prices are in GBP. Any applicable VAT treatment will be disclosed in the quotation and invoice. Small fixed-price bookings are paid in full before testing starts. For custom engagements, milestones or deposits may differ as agreed in writing. We will issue an invoice with payment instructions. No card details should be sent to us by email. We will not start work on the agreed booking before payment is confirmed.

## 6. Dates and delivery
The parties agree a realistic testing and delivery window in writing. The delivery date depends on a working build, necessary instructions and payment being received on time. Any material delay or agreed scope change will be discussed promptly.

## 7. Cancellation and refunds
A client seeking to cancel or reschedule should contact business@ki10games.com promptly. Before work begins, the parties will agree a fair resolution based on the actual costs and applicable legal rights. Once work begins, amounts due for work properly performed and the treatment of any unperformed portion are determined by applicable law and the individual booking agreement. Nothing in these terms excludes statutory consumer cancellation or refund rights where they apply. **Before using this clause with individual hobbyists/consumers, obtain advice on the relevant pre-contract information, 14-day cancellation rights and any request to start a service during the cancellation period.**

## 8. Confidentiality, builds and security
Each party will treat the other's non-public project and commercial information as confidential, use it only for the engagement and not disclose it except where reasonably necessary to deliver the work, or required by law. We will not publish builds, screenshots, bug reports, game footage or client names as portfolio examples without permission. The parties may also enter into a separate NDA. We will agree a secure method of transferring sensitive builds and credentials, and a reasonable retention/deletion period for files after completion.

## 9. Ownership of work
The client retains all ownership of its games, builds and other supplied materials. Once paid, the client may use and share the delivered bug reports internally and with collaborators working on that game. Ki10 retains its pre-existing reporting methods and templates but will not reuse the client's confidential findings or assets in promotional material without permission.

## 10. Questions, complaints and legal rights
Any concerns about a report or service should be raised promptly at business@ki10games.com so we can attempt to resolve them. Nothing in these terms limits liability where the law prevents that, including liability for fraud or negligence causing death or personal injury. Any further liability limitations must be reviewed professionally before use; this draft does not impose a blanket exclusion.

## 11. Governing law
Subject to mandatory consumer protections that may apply, the agreement is intended to be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
