Other ways to play

On this website (above): nothing to install. Any screen with a web browser becomes the TV, and phones join with the game code over the internet, so family far away can join too.

The Quizlo app on a laptop (Windows, Mac or Linux), a Fire TV Stick, an Android TV box or an Android phone/tablet: runs over your home Wi-Fi, and doesn't need the internet once your quizzes are on it. Phones join by scanning the code on the TV, just the same.

Getting it on the TV: plug a laptop in with HDMI, run the app on a Fire TV Stick, AirPlay from a Mac or iPad, cast a Chrome tab to a Chromecast, or open the TV page in a smart TV's own browser.

Quizzes you write here work with every version: send them from the quiz master page, or save the quiz file and open it in the app.
