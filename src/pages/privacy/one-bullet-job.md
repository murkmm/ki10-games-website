_Last updated_: October 8, 2026

The One Bullet Job is made by Ki10 Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It only covers the game. For our website, see the [Ki10 Games privacy policy](/privacy).

## No accounts

The game has no sign-in and doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.

## Your progress stays on your phone

Stars, medals, unlocked outfits, settings and saved runs are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Daily heist leaderboard (optional)

When you finish today's daily heist, the game sends our leaderboard server:

- a random ID created by the game on your device, not linked to your name or Google account;
- the date;
- the stars you earned;
- how many actions you took.

The server turns the random ID into a crew name such as "SILENT FOX 482", so other players can see your rank. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes network data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. We don't store IP addresses with scores.

You can switch the leaderboard off in **Options → Sound & Data → Daily leaderboard**. We may delete old leaderboard days at any time.

## Advertising

The game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address and information about how you interact with ads.

In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in **Options → Sound & Data → Privacy & ad choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

You can read more about [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Contact and deletion requests

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at [contact@ki10games.com](mailto:contact@ki10games.com).

To have your leaderboard entries removed, open the game's challenges screen, note your crew name, and email it to us at the address above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
